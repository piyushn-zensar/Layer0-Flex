--- SCOPE ---
Furnish, install and commission a UPS system providing conditioned and redundant
backup power to data center equipment racks. Delivered as a fully operational,
code-compliant power infrastructure solution.

--- TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS ---
- UPS: 45-kVA online double-conversion UPS system
- Load served: ten (10) data center equipment racks
- Distribution: one dedicated power distribution cabinet
- Branch circuits: twenty-four (24) 208V power whips
- Rack power: rack-mounted PDUs
- Safety: Emergency Power Off (EPO) switch
- Earthing: grounding and bonding
- Installation environment: work performed under a non-plenum raised floor
- Commissioning: all required testing and commissioning included

--- COMPLIANCE ---
Code-compliant installation (NEC / NFPA 70 applicable to 208V branch distribution
and EPO requirements).

--- NOT SPECIFIED IN THIS RFQ ---
No medium-voltage switchgear, no utility interconnection, no liquid cooling, and
no rack density in kW are specified. Scope is limited to UPS, distribution cabinet,
branch circuits and rack PDUs.
