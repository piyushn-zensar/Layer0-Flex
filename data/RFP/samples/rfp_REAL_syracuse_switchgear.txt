--- SCOPE ---
Manufacture, furnish, deliver and test a Metal Clad Switchgear lineup and accessories.

--- TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS ---
- Equipment: One 15kV two-section Metal Clad Switchgear lineup
- Breaker positions: 18 x 15kV vacuum circuit breaker positions with protective relays and controls
- Arc resistance: Arc-resistant Type 2B, meeting or exceeding IEEE C37.20.7
- Metering: 2 revenue meter positions
- Isolation: Two sets of 2 x 15kV isolation switches
- Instrument transformers: bus VTs and multi-ratio CTs
- Bus configuration: Two bus sections with tie breaker
- Incoming supply: Two 13.2 kV utility feeders from National Grid, underground cable connection
- Cable terminations: NEMA four-hole pad and insulating boots, up to two 750 MCM 15kV cables per phase
- Utility compartment: Separate lockable utility incoming cable termination compartment
- Grounding: 1 inch diameter ground ball per phase; 5/8 inch x 24 inch copper ground bail
- Future capacity: Future bays provided fully functional without VCBs included
- Enclosure: Switchgear mounted within a fully enclosed Equipment Building (supplied by others)

--- COMPLIANCE STANDARDS ---
IEEE C37.20.2 (Metal-Clad Switchgear), IEEE C37.20.7 (Internal Arcing Faults),
IEEE C37.04 / C37.06 / C37.09 / C37.010 (AC High-Voltage Circuit Breakers),
IEEE C37.90 / C37.90.1 (Relays and Surge Withstand), IEEE C57.13 (Instrument Transformers),
IEEE C2 (National Electrical Safety Code), NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code),
ANSI, NEMA SG-4 / SG-5 / SG-6, ASTM, OSHA 1910

--- COMMERCIAL / DELIVERY ---
- Pricing basis: Lump sum covering manufacturing, furnishing, delivering, testing
- Also required in cost proposal: special tools; drawings, instructions and test reports in English;
  final drawings in AutoCAD format; maintenance equipment and spare parts; start-up and training
- Bidder must submit a schedule covering drawings, factory shipment, site delivery,
  overall lead time ARO, and field works
- Field technical service: optional pricing line
- Participation goals: 30% MWBE, 6% SDVOB (New York State Executive Law Articles 15-A and 17-B)

--- NOT SPECIFIED IN THIS RFP ---
Rack density, cooling requirements, IT capacity in MW, and data-hall power architecture
are not applicable to this scope — this is a facility/utility electrical procurement,
not a data centre build.
