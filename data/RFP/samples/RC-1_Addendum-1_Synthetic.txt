ADDENDUM NO. 1
RC-1 AI Campus: Grid-to-Chip Request for Proposal (Synthetic)
Date of Addendum: October 23, 2026

This addendum adds requirements to Lot 4 (rack power) of the RFP. Requirement IDs continue the Lot 4
numbering of the RFP.

A. ADDITIONS
1. Lot 4, after L4.12: add the following as L4.13, Compute tray power conversion.
"L4.13 Each compute tray shall convert the 54 VDC in-rack bus, which varies between 40 VDC and 60 VDC,
to a 12 VDC intermediate bus for the tray's boards. The converter shall deliver at least 1,500 W of
continuous output power per tray."

2. Lot 4, after L4.13: add the following as L4.14, Compute tray converter telemetry and efficiency.
"L4.14 The compute tray converters shall report telemetry over PMBus and shall have a typical efficiency
of at least 97% at full load."

3. Lot 4, after L4.14: add the following as L4.15, Compute tray converter size.
"L4.15 Each compute tray converter shall fit a board area of at most 60 mm by 40 mm, with a height of at
most 15 mm."

All other terms of the RFP remain unchanged.
