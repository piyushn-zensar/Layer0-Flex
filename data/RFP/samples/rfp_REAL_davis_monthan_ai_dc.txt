IMPORTANT CLASSIFICATION NOTE:
This is a LAND LEASE solicitation, not an equipment procurement. SpinCo would not
bid on this directly; the winning developer becomes the buyer of power and cooling
infrastructure. It is included as a fixture because it defines the grid-interface
requirements of exactly the kind of site SpinCo equips.

--- SCOPE ---
Lease of underutilized federal land for private commercial AI data center
development, including associated infrastructure and generation to support
energy reliability and resilience.

--- QUALIFYING PROJECT THRESHOLDS (per Executive Order 14318) ---
- New electrical load: greater than 100 MW
- Capital expenditure: minimum $500 million
- Purpose: dedicated to AI inference, training, simulation, or synthetic data generation
- Davis-Monthan parcel: up to 620 acres, sited for large scale data center use (500 MW+)

--- INFRASTRUCTURE TECHNOLOGIES SOUGHT ---
- Onsite energy generation (e.g. natural gas, small modular reactors)
- Battery energy storage systems
- Fuel storage
- Upgraded electrical distribution infrastructure
- Complementary microgrid capability
- Where a developer proposes a dedicated energy source, the Government requires
  first right of refusal of power during a grid outage

--- BIDDER QUALIFICATION ---
At least three completed AI data center projects drawing over 100 MW of new
contracted power, developed within the last 3 years.

--- NOT SPECIFIED ---
Rack density, cooling architecture, switchgear ratings and rack-level power
architecture are left to the developer; this document defines site and grid-interface
requirements only.
