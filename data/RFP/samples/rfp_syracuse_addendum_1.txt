Request for Proposals - Switchgear Procurement, RFP Reference # 2023-20
Syracuse Regional Airport Authority
Date of Addendum: September 5, 2023

This addendum modifies the Request for Proposals as follows. Page numbers refer to the
RFP document. Specification means 22186.00-SPC-E01, Specification for Metal Clad
Switchgear.

A. MODIFICATIONS
1. Section 1.9, Submission Due Date, page 5: replace "09/15/2023 by 4:00PM" with
"09/29/2023 by 4:00PM".

2. Specification Section 1.1.E.1, page 55: replace "eighteen (18) 15kV vacuum circuit
breaker positions" with "twenty (20) 15kV vacuum circuit breaker positions".

B. ADDITIONS
3. Specification Section 1.1.E, page 56: add the following as item 11. "The switchgear
lineup shall be seismically qualified for the site, and the MANUFACTURER shall provide
the seismic qualification report with the submittals."

4. Specification Part 1, after Section 1.6: add the following as Section 1.7, Warranty.
"The MANUFACTURER shall warrant the switchgear lineup against defects in materials and
workmanship for sixty (60) months from the date of energization."

C. DELETIONS
5. Specification Section 1.1.D.3, page 55: the sentence "In addition to other Submittal
requirements, final drawings shall be submitted as AutoCAD format files." is deleted in
its entirety.

D. QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS
6. Question: Is arc-resistant Type 2B construction required? Answer: Yes, arc-resistant
Type 2B construction is required as specified in Section 1.1.E.2; no change.

E. GENERAL
