AMX Switchboards (UL891)

AMX switchboards are modular, low-voltage power distribution assemblies tested and verified to UL891. Designed for data centers, AI/HPC facilities, and critical infrastructure, AMX switchboards deliver high-density power distribution with the safety, uptime, and serviceability that critical operations demand. Busbars rated from 1200A to 6000A, breaker-based selective coordination, and a compact footprint make AMX a reliable foundation for scalable electrical distribution.
 
The AMX platform is built around modular, standardized sections that support design flexibility without compromising consistency. Breaker- and meter-agnostic integration, Modbus TCP/IP and Modbus RTU connectivity, and customer-defined metering points allow seamless alignment with site standards and building management systems.

Key features of AMX Switchboards:

  • Modular, bolted construction supports scalability, phased deployment, and future expansion
  • Rear isolated, tin-plated copper busbars rated from 1200A to 6000A
  • Dead-front construction with segregated ACB compartments and guarded MCCB terminals
  • Fixed or withdrawable ACB configurations; group-mounted MCCBs with optimized stacking density
  • Mechanical interlocks, kirk key systems, and LOTO-ready breaker mechanisms
  • Meter-agnostic architecture with Modbus TCP/IP and Modbus RTU connectivity
  • Front access serviceability for operation, metering, and maintenance
  • Tested and verified to UL891; OSHPD compliant seismic options available (ICC-ES AC156)
  • Manufacturing follows ISO 9001 quality management system

Enhanced benefits:

  • Selective coordination and fully rated bus system maintain uptime by isolating faults to affected circuits only
  • Compact footprint and high stacking density reduce electrical room space and associated civil costs
  • Natural convection cooling eliminates fans, lowering system complexity and long-term maintenance requirements
  • Breaker- and meter-agnostic design provides supply chain resilience and alignment with diverse site standards
  • Seamless BMS/EMS integration delivers real-time visibility into power quality and system status
